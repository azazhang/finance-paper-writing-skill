# Scholarly Finance Voice Calibration

**Target:** the writing conventions of research articles in JF, JFE, RFS, and closely related scholarly finance journals. Calibrate to the discipline's economic reasoning and evidentiary standards. Personal authorial mannerisms are secondary.

## Core conventions

- **Economic object:** Write about firms, investors, intermediaries, financial contracts, asset prices, incentives, or allocation. Make clear who chooses or bears risk, and why the question matters.
- **Unresolved question:** Establish a genuine empirical puzzle, theoretical tension, competing force, or missing economic margin. Build from the actual study; do not invent a controversy.
- **Design and inference:** Explain the economically informative comparison, variation, or equilibrium restriction. State what is identified or predicted with the language supported by the method.
- **Quantitative interpretation:** Give magnitudes and economically meaningful benchmarks, including their units, timing, and precision where relevant. Tell readers what the estimates imply for financing, behavior, prices, or welfare.
- **Contribution:** Explain what the nearest scholarship already teaches and which additional economic inference this paper earns. Novel data or methodology matter through the question they help answer.
- **Restraint:** Use confident, measured finance prose. Give limitations their proper scope. Avoid promotional adjectives, formulaic contrasts, generic academic essays, and passages that explain the research workflow rather than the economy.

## Publication register versus generic AI or research-memo prose

A finance research article makes **economic agents, contracts, financial constraints, identifying comparisons, and implications** its grammatical and intellectual subjects. A memo reports the author's activities; a generic essay announces significance. In a manuscript, translate both into an economic claim that is answerable to evidence.

**Research-memo sentence:** "The final analysis produced a stable main coefficient after reworking the matching pipeline."

**Scholarly version, if supported:** "Within-borrower comparisons show that banks experiencing larger deposit outflows reduce credit more sharply than other lenders to the same firm."

**Generic AI sentence:** "These multifaceted findings highlight the critical importance of financial flexibility for stakeholders."

**Scholarly version, if supported:** "The investment gap is largest when debt matures within one year, linking covenant proximity to firms' refinancing exposure."

The publication versions identify the research object, comparison, and financial-economic interpretation. They are **illustrations**, not actual estimates or permission to infer causation. Material details of construction, model calibration, software, or sample assembly remain appropriate in the design or replication materials when scientifically necessary. Keep the *process of writing or debugging the paper* out of reader-facing prose; see [editorial-diagnostics.md](editorial-diagnostics.md).

## What each section should accomplish

**Abstract:** State a consequential question, what allows it to be addressed, the central finding or theorem, and its financial-economic meaning. Connect the ideas in natural prose; avoid a list of regressions.

**Introduction:** Move from economic motivation to the question, the informative comparison, headline evidence, interpretation, and contribution. Develop the argument itself; an editorial outline is insufficient.

**Framework or theory:** Make the agents, constraints, incentives, predictions, and comparative statics intelligible. Explain the economic force that produces the result. Theoretical and empirical papers legitimately use different structures.

**Data and design:** Describe constructs, sample, observation unit, timing, identifying variation, and relevant assumptions in ordinary finance terminology. Include technical details because they bear on inference.

**Results:** Give each principal exhibit an economic job. Explain the decisive comparisons, economic magnitudes, uncertainty, and what they establish about the mechanism or alternatives. Organize paragraphs by the argument, not by table columns.

**Conclusion:** Integrate the supported answer and its implications for financial behavior, pricing, or allocation, with material boundaries stated accurately.

## Learn from published scholarship

The following published work illustrates distinct *rhetorical jobs*. These are examples to consult selectively, not mandatory reading or source text to copy.

- [Gourier, Phalippou, and Westerfield, *Capital Commitment* (JF, 2024)](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13382): starts with an operative financial contract, develops a trade-off, and reports its portfolio and welfare implications.
- [Agarwal, Ruenzi, and Weigert, *Unobserved Performance of Hedge Funds* (JF, 2024)](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13368): defines a performance construct and links it to risk-adjusted returns and investor behavior. The lesson is to use new measurement to reveal an economically meaningful price or allocation question.
- [Diamond, Jiang, and Ma, *The Reserve Supply Channel of Unconventional Monetary Policy* (JFE, 2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2024.103887): connects a surprising bank-lending result to magnitudes and an intermediary balance-sheet channel.
- [*The Death of a Regulator: Strict Supervision, Bank Lending, and Business Activity* (JFE, 2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2024.103871): links an institutional supervisory change to a concrete lending comparison and subsequent business activity; the writing makes the economic channel intelligible.
- [Dangl and Zechner, *Debt Maturity and the Dynamics of Leverage* (RFS, 2021)](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa148): develops a model–fact tension and explains how debt refinancing changes leverage dynamics.
- [Lettau and Pelger, *Factors That Fit the Time Series and Cross-Section of Stock Returns* (RFS, 2020)](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa020): motivates a method through asset-pricing restrictions and economically interpretable portfolio outcomes.

**How agents should use exemplars:** Infer the example's rhetorical job—economic motivation, the design-to-inference link, how numerical evidence is interpreted, or how the contribution is stated. Then write original prose from the **current paper's actual evidence**, choosing the conventions appropriate to its research type. For instance, theory can emphasize equilibrium mechanisms and comparative statics; observational asset pricing can emphasize prediction, risk adjustment, and economic magnitude. An exact personal voice match is neither possible nor necessary in blind-reviewed finance research.

This method adapts [Manish Jha's *AI for Finance Research*](https://mjha91.github.io/pdf/AIFinanceResearch.pdf): derive **stance, evidence rules, and style patterns** from effective scholarly passages. The agent must take responsibility for the intellectually demanding introduction, contribution, and interpretation, then check numerical claims against exhibits and maintain the same scholarly voice across revisions. A separate voice-profile exercise, quote collection, or round of AI referee simulation is unnecessary. Preserve effective author-approved terminology and rhythm when consistent with the publication standard.
