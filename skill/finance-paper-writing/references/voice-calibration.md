# Voice Calibration: Scholarly Financial-Economics Publication Style

## Governing standard and activation

**Calibrate first to the scholarly publication register of financial economics.** A finance paper should read as a research contribution to other financial economists: it develops a consequential economic question, makes the relevant theoretical or institutional forces intelligible, states what the evidence establishes, interprets the key comparison, and explains the incremental understanding. This disciplinary standard takes precedence over an author's personal prose habits or a language model's default eloquence.

Read and apply this reference for **substantive academic finance-manuscript drafting and revision** (abstracts, introductions, results, theory, literature positioning, and conclusions). For a one-sentence factual or grammatical correction, maintain this established register without initiating new reference reading or a style exercise. GPT-family agents and Codex also follow the mandatory [anti-defensive-prose](anti-defensive-prose.md) guidance on every prose edit.

The writer must construct the assigned manuscript passage itself. Learning publication style is part of producing the paper; it does not authorize a new literature-search campaign, style dossier, preliminary outline, multiple mock referees, or approval stage. Use the manuscript, established evidence, and these distilled conventions directly.

## What distinguishes scholarly finance prose from generic academic writing

1. **Economic object first.** Ground claims in a recognizable choice, friction, risk, security, financial contract, price, or allocation. Explain whose behavior or valuation is involved and why the distinction matters.
2. **A reason the answer is unresolved.** Develop a puzzle, theoretically ambiguous response, unmeasured margin, conflicting prediction, or important empirical uncertainty when the actual study supports it. The motivation should set up the particular comparison the paper delivers.
3. **Design as a source of inference.** Describe the comparison or identifying variation in the language of the economic question. Specify what is held constant, the timing, unit of observation, and what the evidence can distinguish when those features matter.
4. **Quantitative discipline.** Communicate the principal estimate or theoretical magnitude with a recoverable unit, denominator, baseline, horizon, and precision. Economic importance is established by interpretation against an appropriate benchmark, not a string of significance labels.
5. **Mechanism and competing explanations.** Explain the economic forces linking the result to the question. Distinguish a theoretical implication, mechanism-consistent pattern, prediction, and identified causal effect. Treat alternative interpretations as live when the design leaves them open.
6. **Incremental knowledge.** Represent the nearest scholarship accurately and explain which unresolved economic question, inferential margin, mechanism, or prediction the paper advances. Method or dataset innovations matter because of the question they answer.
7. **Restrained, confident exposition.** Use substantive verbs and plain financial-economic language. Avoid promotional adjectives, generic importance claims, model-generated aphorisms, defensive scaffolding, and arbitrary rhetoric. A well-founded qualification belongs exactly where it changes the inference.

A published paper is not a single stylistic template. Equilibrium theory, structural estimation, reduced-form causal identification, descriptive measurement, and predictive asset pricing differ in their inferential vocabulary and exposition. Apply the common norms to the assigned design.

## By rhetorical job

### Abstract

Build one connected account of the question, approach or setting, result, and economic meaning. For empirical work, prioritize one or a few economically interpretable estimates; for theory, state the central economic force, result, and condition. A novel model or text measure is introduced through the financial-economic question it resolves. Keep procedural details and secondary robustness claims in their appropriate sections.

### Introduction and contribution

Open with the consequential financial-economic problem and explain the source of uncertainty or tension. Establish what the paper studies, why its comparison is informative, and the decisive finding. Develop the answer and its implications over connected paragraphs. The contribution should identify what the closest existing studies explain, what remains open, and what this paper's evidence adds. The writer is expected to **write the actual contribution**, using the verified study assets and available literature. A set of possible framings or referee-style critiques is not a completed introduction.

### Economic framework and theory

State the economic agents, incentives, constraints, information, and equilibrium forces at a level the reader needs to interpret the result. Explain a proposition's economic content, parameter conditions, and comparative statics in prose. Show why the result emerges. Mathematical precision and an accessible economic interpretation belong together. A theoretical paper can legitimately lead with a sharp equilibrium result; it need not imitate an empirical introduction.

### Data, design, and measurement

Present economic constructs, institutional setting, sample eligibility, unit and timing, variation, and inferential limits. Put the conceptual reason a measurement captures its intended object ahead of classifier, software, or storage details. Technical detail earns its place by allowing the reader to understand or assess the inference. Do not describe the manuscript as a development pipeline.

### Results

Give each main exhibit a question and an economic answer. Interpret the decisive coefficient, spread, elasticity, or comparative static using meaningful scales and precision; explain its relation to the mechanism, the identifying comparison, and alternatives. Organize a sequence of exhibits around the developing argument, not table numbers or estimation chronology. Carry forward consistent constructs, cohorts, timing, and magnitudes from earlier sections.

### Conclusion

State the supported insight and what it changes about financial behavior, prices, or allocation. The conclusion integrates the paper's economic answer and its consequential scope. It does not accumulate caveats, advertise technical effort, or introduce a new research agenda as a substitute for the finding.

## Scholarly register: practical sentence and paragraph decisions

- Prefer **firms invest, lenders price, investors rebalance, banks extend credit, portfolios earn returns, and contracts allocate control** to generic subjects such as “the research,” “the analysis,” or “this work” when the economic agent is available.
- Use precise terminology: *basis points, percentage points, excess returns, factor-adjusted alpha, duration, rollover exposure, leverage, debt maturity, borrower demand, lender credit supply, conditional prediction, event-window response*. Define units and constructs at first meaningful use.
- A paragraph advances a proposition or economic comparison. Its design facts and quantitative evidence earn the interpretation. Transitions move the argument: a new force, test, comparison, boundary, or implication.
- “We show,” “we find,” and “I document” are normal scholarly expressions when the evidence supports them, and authors may prefer an impersonal construction. Choose the form appropriate to the paper, coauthor convention, and claim. Never use declarative confidence to cover a weak or unverified inference.
- Place citations near the attributed research claim. Do not define a contribution by listing names and datasets; explain what the neighboring research teaches and the economic margin advanced here.
- Treat caveats as inferential information. State a real limitation when it changes interpretation; do not open the core argument with imagined objections. Use the strict GPT/Codex anti-defensive guidance where applicable.
- Avoid generic “research is important” prose, dramatic hooks, unsupported “first” claims, mechanical three-item lists, and essay-like commentary. Strong finance prose is dense with financial-economic content yet remains readable.
- Study the *function* of an exemplar's paragraph, not its distinctive phrases. Published papers can themselves contain constructions the current draft should avoid; extract the successful reasoning pattern rather than reproduce every feature.

## Verified published exemplars: annotated reading guide

These references come from published articles in **The Journal of Finance (JF), Journal of Financial Economics (JFE), and The Review of Financial Studies (RFS)**. The observations below are about the cited **abstracts**, and in the explicitly identified cases **introductions**. They are source-grounded rhetorical annotations, not endorsements of every wording choice. No published passage is reproduced here.

**1. Theory, economic stakes, quantitative welfare:**
[Elise Gourier, Ludovic Phalippou, and Mark M. Westerfield, “Capital Commitment,” JF (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13382).
The abstract identifies a specific institutional requirement in private markets, presents a dynamic allocation model, reports portfolio and welfare implications, and explains counterintuitive effects of liquidity and diversification. **Transfer:** begin from the economically operative contract, show the model's key trade-off, and interpret the welfare or pricing implication.

**2. Measurement linked to priced information and magnitude:**
[Vikas Agarwal, Stefan Ruenzi, and Florian Weigert, “Unobserved Performance of Hedge Funds,” JF (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13368).
The abstract defines its performance construct operationally, reports the risk-adjusted return difference with an annualized magnitude, relates it to hedge-fund activities, and explains delayed investor responses. **Transfer:** define a new measure through the financial object and show what its financial consequences teach; a measure by itself does not carry the contribution.

**3. Monetary transmission, result and channel:**
[William Diamond, Zhengyang Jiang, and Yiming Ma, “The Reserve Supply Channel of Unconventional Monetary Policy,” JFE (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2024.103887).
The abstract poses a counterintuitive relation between reserves and bank lending, identifies a structural approach and demand elasticities, gives loan-rate and lending magnitudes, and explains bank balance-sheet costs as the mechanism. **Transfer:** state the economic result and magnitude early, then link the identifying or structural evidence to a concrete financial intermediary margin.

**4. Institutional variation and causal chain:**
[“The Death of a Regulator: Strict Supervision, Bank Lending, and Business Activity,” JFE (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2024.103871).
The abstract uses a supervisory transition to motivate the empirical comparison, reports the change in small-business lending, and relates heterogeneity to changes in bank management. **Transfer:** explain why institutional variation identifies the object of interest and develop the economic channel from evidence rather than simply declaring a natural experiment.

**5. Theoretical puzzle and model mechanism across the introduction:**
[Thomas Dangl and Josef Zechner, “Debt Maturity and the Dynamics of Leverage,” RFS (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa148).
The introduction contrasts familiar dynamic-capital-structure models' implications with observed leverage reductions, then develops a mechanism involving the refinancing of maturing debt. **Transfer:** establish a sharp model–fact discrepancy, explain the economically relevant friction, and derive what the theory resolves.

**6. Empirical asset-pricing method tied to an economic objective:**
[Martin Lettau and Markus Pelger, “Factors That Fit the Time Series and Cross-Section of Stock Returns,” RFS (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa020).
The abstract links a factor-extraction method to pricing errors, portfolios, out-of-sample Sharpe ratios, and redundant characteristic information. **Transfer:** discuss methodological innovation through the asset-pricing restriction or economic quantity it improves, with a comparison that communicates magnitude.

These examples cover different kinds of scholarly contributions. They are **anchors for calibration**, not a required article-reading list. The distilled rules above should ordinarily suffice. Open an exemplar only when it clarifies a particular unresolved writing choice; use its actual text, not a generic assumption about prestigious-journal prose. Do not copy distinctive sentences or recycle another paper's claims.

## Examples and an optional style calibration

Use [synthetic manuscript examples](../examples/before-after.md) to inspect original, connected paragraphs for abstract, introduction, results, literature positioning, and other research types. Those numbers and settings are explicitly illustrative. They demonstrate construction, not source authority.

For a substantial draft, compare the **rhetorical job** of its affected sections against the publication conventions above: Does the opening motivate an economic question? Does the main comparison answer it? Are magnitudes interpreted? Does the contribution explain new knowledge? Revise the actual prose. No written calibration matrix, scoring cycle, extra agent, or benchmark-corpus collection is required.

## Secondary: preserve individual author preferences

When effective author- or coauthor-approved finance prose is already present, preserve terminology, first-person convention, natural transitions, and sentence rhythm insofar as they remain consistent with rigorous scholarly publication style. The author’s established intellectual argument and approved formulations deserve particular respect. Personal stylistic consistency serves the paper's clarity; it does not override sound financial-economic register, verifiable evidence, or the requested revision.

A separately documented personal voice profile is optional **only when explicitly requested**. Do not infer an individual’s style from synthetic skill examples or impose an unrelated famous author's mannerisms. Blind-review readers should recognize a coherent scholarly finance paper regardless of the authors' identities.
