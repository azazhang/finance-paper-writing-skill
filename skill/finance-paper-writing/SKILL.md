# Finance Paper Writing

**Produce a compelling scholarly financial-economics paper.** The manuscript must advance an economically consequential question, an evidence-supported answer, and a clear contribution to knowledge. Write in the publication register of finance research, with the strongest quantitative or theoretical evidence central to the argument. A technically correct inventory of estimates is insufficient.

## Own the assigned manuscript work

Read the current manuscript, the author's instructions, and the established results needed for the task. Draft and revise the actual prose, including introductions, contributions, and economic interpretations when assigned. Resolve writing problems directly; do not return only critiques, outlines, planning documents, or reviewer objections. Preserve the agreed research question and supported evidence. Further data collection or estimation requires its own justification and authorization.

Scale reading to the task: a small correction requires its sentence and context; a section rewrite requires the section and relevant transitions; substantial whole-paper work requires the assembled manuscript and principal exhibits. Proceed with available evidence and identify only consequential unresolved scientific facts.

**For GPT-family models and Codex:** read and apply [anti-defensive-prose.md](references/anti-defensive-prose.md) on **every finance-manuscript prose edit**, even a single sentence. Apply it when the model family is uncertain. Other models may consult it as needed. Its purpose is better writing, with no separate compliance ritual.

## Construct the economic argument

Identify the financial behavior, price, contract, friction, incentive, or allocation at issue. Explain why the answer is uncertain and economically important; use competing predictions only when the actual research supports them. State the paper's main answer in terms the evidence can bear, identify the decisive comparison or theoretical result, interpret it economically, and explain what it changes relative to the closest genuine research.

Treat measurement, algorithms, datasets, and specifications as means of answering the economic question. A claim can be descriptive, predictive, mechanism-consistent, or causal when its design warrants that language. Falsifiability means evidence can challenge the claim; causal identification is not a universal requirement.

## Write like a finance scholar

Use [voice-calibration.md](references/voice-calibration.md) for the core journal-writing standard: economic objects, explanatory mechanisms, finance terminology, precise comparisons, measured confidence, and connected paragraphs. Individual author preferences are secondary. The introduction earns the question and contribution; the abstract connects the question, evidence, and implication; results explain what the main exhibits teach; the conclusion closes on the supported insight. Avoid generic importance rhetoric, method-centered exposition, coefficient catalogues, and artificial AI eloquence.

Give each paragraph a specific economic job. Advance a point, support it with the relevant design or evidence, and interpret its significance. Preserve conceptual contrasts and genuine limitations when they affect inference. Use the neighboring literature fairly; if a comparator fact is uncertain, verify it when feasible and write the narrower supported contribution rather than abandoning the draft. See [closest-paper-positioning.md](references/closest-paper-positioning.md) only for substantive literature-positioning work.

## Make claims answerable to evidence

Check changed estimates, magnitudes, reference groups, units, denominators, horizons, precision, exhibit references, and literature attributions against the current results and primary sources. Select main-text exhibits for their contribution to the economic claim; place secondary diagnostics in the appendix where appropriate. Interpret informative null or unfavorable findings and narrow claims they materially affect. See [evidence-and-claims.md](references/evidence-and-claims.md) when choosing or interpreting evidence; use [text-measure-papers.md](references/text-measure-papers.md) only for textual constructs.

## Validate the manuscript agentically

**The writing agent is the validator.** Read the resulting prose as a scholarly finance reader and repair weaknesses in the actual manuscript. For substantial whole-paper work, read the **assembled paper from abstract through conclusion**, preferably the rendered PDF with its exhibits. Confirm the question, design, results, economic interpretation, and contribution agree across sections. Fix stale claims, incompatible samples, inconsistent estimates, weak transitions, repetitive or stitched-together paragraphs, and departures from finance-publication style. For local edits, verify the affected passage and its necessary context.

A fresh independent reader may follow **after** the writer has repaired the manuscript, when requested or useful and available. Give that reader a narrow comprehension assignment; implement justified corrections and stop. No serial referee rounds, scores, pass thresholds, scripts, regex scans, or record-keeping systems are part of writing validation. [independent-reader.md](references/independent-reader.md) describes the writer's integrated reading and optional cold reader.

## Deliver

Finish the requested manuscript change, verify affected evidence and rendering where relevant, and report what economic argument or passage improved. Provide the revised manuscript or PDF when applicable. Scholarly quality is judged from the paper itself, including its intellectual contribution, coherence, evidence, and inferential credibility.
