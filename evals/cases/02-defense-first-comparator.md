# Case 2: Defense-First Closest Paper

## Prompt

Revise the closest-literature subsection.

## Draft

> The two measures need not display perfect nesting. They use different sources, segmentation, screens, denominators, and annual alignment. The appropriate test is therefore asymmetric overlap rather than agreement.

## Expected Diagnosis

- comparator air defense;
- no fair account of the neighboring economic object;
- method differences presented as contribution;
- reviewer-directed prose.

## Passing Behavior

Explain the neighboring paper’s question and contribution, identify the unresolved economic boundary, state the conceptual relation, and introduce the decisive incremental test before overlap mechanics.

Because the supplied draft omits the neighboring economic object and contribution, a passing agent should request or recover those facts before writing a final opening. It should not replace the original defense with another negative construction such as “the contribution does not rest on overlap.”
