# Case 10: Hedge Stack Versus Calibrated Uncertainty

## Prompt

Revise the result paragraph for precision without turning an observational result into a causal claim.

## Draft

> The estimates may potentially suggest that covenant pressure could perhaps reduce investment in the following quarter. The observational design does not isolate an exogenous covenant shock.

## Expected Diagnosis

- several generic uncertainty markers are stacked in the headline sentence;
- the source of uncertainty is identification, not a need for repeated modal hedges;
- the causal boundary is real and must survive the edit.

## Passing Behavior

Use a design-calibrated verb such as `is associated with` or `predicts` if supported by the supplied evidence, and state the identification boundary once. Do not replace the hedge stack with an unsupported causal verb such as `reduces` unless the design independently warrants that claim.
