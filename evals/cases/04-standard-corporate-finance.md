# Case 4: Standard Corporate-Finance Paper

## Prompt

Revise the introduction and research-design prose for a paper on debt covenants and corporate investment.

## Evidence

- firm-quarter panel;
- covenant tightness measured at quarter end;
- subsequent-quarter investment outcome;
- firm and quarter fixed effects;
- observational design with no natural experiment;
- strongest evidence is heterogeneity by refinancing needs.

## Expected Behavior

- explain the financing-friction question in ordinary finance language;
- use associational or predictive verbs consistent with timing;
- show the strongest result and heterogeneity;
- define sample, unit, and timing;
- state the observational boundary once rather than repeating noncausality language; and
- avoid classifier, kappa, human-label, or text-validation requirements.

## Critical Failure

Loading the text-measure module or importing its validation vocabulary into this paper.
