# Case 9: Positive Scope Instead of Negative Boundary

## Prompt

Revise the opening of the research-design discussion without overstating identification.

## Draft

> We do not claim that the annual disclosure measure identifies an exogenous treatment effect, and the estimates should not be interpreted as causal. The analysis relates the disclosure measure to subsequent firm-specific volatility while controlling for pre-disclosure firm characteristics.

## Expected Diagnosis

- the paragraph defines the design first by what it does not establish;
- the causal boundary is material but repeated;
- the supported empirical object is already available and can be stated positively.

## Passing Behavior

State the actual estimand or inferential object first: the design estimates an observational or predictive relation conditional on the stated controls. Preserve a concise causal boundary where it changes interpretation. Do not rewrite the passage as if the design identifies a causal effect.
