# Scholarly Manuscript Diagnosis: Research Prose Versus Research Memo

**Purpose:** Help the writing agent recognize and **repair** failures of finance-journal exposition during drafting and the integrated manuscript reading. Apply to substantive writing and whole-paper review; a small edit needs only the relevant passage. This is an editorial reading guide, with no scoring, keyword scan, compliance form, or additional agent required.

## Keep development activity out of reader-facing prose

The manuscript explains **the study and its evidence**. Internal execution history belongs in the research workspace. Do not import:

- local file paths, folder structures, version numbers or labels such as "v6-positive," run IDs, commit hashes, logs, generated-output filenames, or debugging history;
- agent/assistant roles, approval processes, prompts, passes, review packets, adjudication rounds, and descriptions of who instructed a model to change a paragraph;
- software metaphors such as *pipeline*, *deployment*, *backbone*, or *governance* when they merely describe the author's workflow and convey no financial-economic or reproducibility fact;
- classifier-development status, internal training stages, or "teacher/student" metaphors as the motivation or contribution of a text-measure paper.

**Replace development history with recoverable research facts:** data source and coverage, sample eligibility, timing, construct definition, coding rule, observation unit, design, estimand, estimates, and uncertainty. A method's genuine computational or version details can belong in reproducibility materials or the empirical design when necessary to interpret or reproduce the research. Do not erase substantive methodology.

**Memo-style draft:** "In our latest run, we stabilized the sample filters and generated the final results table, resolving a reviewer concern about maturity definitions."

**Scholarly treatment (when documented):** "The sample includes nonfinancial U.S. borrowers with observed covenant thresholds and debt maturities. Firms near those thresholds invest less over the next four quarters, and the difference is larger when debt matures within one year."

The second passage describes the financial objects and evidence. Verify every factual element against the actual paper; the illustration is not a license to invent results.

## Recognize when a paper sounds like a memo

| Symptoms in the manuscript | Agentic repair |
|---|---|
| "Objective," "status," "what changed," "issue resolved," "next steps," or report-style bullets narrate the project | Write a connected research paragraph around the **economic proposition and evidence**. |
| Paragraphs explain what the author tested, coded, reviewed, or fixed without saying what readers learn | Make the financial behavior, mechanism, or price the subject; interpret the result. |
| An abstract reads like an inventory of coefficients, subsamples, and robustness exercises | Develop one thread from motivating uncertainty to main finding and economic implication. |
| A section opens by anticipating reviewer criticism, defending a comparator, or rehearsing limitations | Establish the supported claim, design, evidence, and its economic scope first; preserve material limitations where they change inference. |
| Generic "important," "novel," "complex," "multifaceted," or "underscores" sentences inflate significance | Specify the affected agents, financial object, magnitude, and incremental knowledge. |
| A methodological or measurement innovation occupies the contribution paragraph | Explain which economic question it makes answerable and what the evidence resolves. |
| Sections resemble independent fragments: different samples, horizons, estimates, or accounts of the mechanism | Read the assembled paper in order, reconcile the claims against the current evidence, and rewrite transitions and stale passages. |
| An old cited result or neighboring-study claim is implied without verification | Verify the actual source; narrow or remove unsupported attribution while delivering the requested argument. |

**The words are clues, not bans.** A research paper can legitimately contain an algorithm, calibration, version, pipeline, or program when it is a scientific object. The issue is the *paragraph's function*, its audience, and whether it establishes a finance result. Swapping synonyms cannot repair memo-like argumentation.

## Calibrate scholarly claims without displaying "calibration" as process language

Choose the inferential verb from the design: *documents* for descriptive facts; *is associated with* for conditional observational relations; *predicts* for properly ordered forecasting; *is consistent with* for a mechanism-compatible pattern; and causal verbs only when identification supports them. Explain precisely what remains uncertain.

In a manuscript, write the actual relationship, estimand, assumptions, and boundaries. Terms such as "claim calibration," "evidence state," "high-confidence finding," "safe phrasing," or "defensive-edit disposition" describe the **agent's editorial process** and usually do not belong in reader-facing prose. Statistical *calibration* is appropriate when it names a genuine estimation or model procedure.

A material limitation belongs where it changes interpretation. A wide confidence interval is uncertainty about magnitude; it does not support an asserted null. A nonexperimental association does not warrant a treatment-effect claim. Prevent both inflated inference and repeated declarations of inability.

## Whole-paper reading: find the visible defects before any independent reader

The writer must encounter the final assembled manuscript, with exhibits where they are cited. Reconstruct the economic question, central comparison, result, contribution, and the inferential scope **from the written paper itself**. Check that sample, horizon, constructs, estimates, causal language, and literature positioning remain consistent from abstract through conclusion. Ask whether each paragraph develops the argument or merely reports an activity. Directly repair genuine problems, then reread the resulting transitions.

An independent cold reader should be evaluating the **author's coherent draft**. It should not be delegated obvious errors left by the writer, nor asked for repeated scored referee rounds.

**Passage-level criterion:** A finance researcher should understand what financial object is studied, what the evidence says, and why the paragraph advances the paper. If the paragraph fails this test, reconstruct or delete it. No automated tool can establish this outcome.
