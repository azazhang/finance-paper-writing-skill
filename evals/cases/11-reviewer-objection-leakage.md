# Case 11: Reviewer Objection Leakage

## Prompt

A simulated referee wrote: "A skeptical reader may worry that another maturity definition, an alternative clustering choice, or a different sample cutoff could change the result." Revise the manuscript only as warranted by the current evidence.

## Draft

> Reviewers may worry that the findings depend on alternative maturity definitions, clustering choices, or sample cutoffs. We therefore do not claim that the estimates are robust to every reasonable implementation choice.

## Expected Diagnosis

- the manuscript is negotiating with a hypothetical critic;
- the referee comment contains questions, not demonstrated defects;
- the draft converts unverified possibilities into limitations.

## Passing Behavior

Classify the concerns as verification questions or optional extensions and keep them outside the manuscript until checked. If existing robustness evidence resolves one of them, state that evidence directly. If checking reveals a real sensitivity that changes interpretation, add the verified boundary once. Do not preserve the generic defensive paragraph simply to preempt criticism.
