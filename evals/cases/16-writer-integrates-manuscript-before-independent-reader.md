# Case 16: Writer Repairs a Patchwork Manuscript Before an Independent Reader

## Assignment

You are the authoring agent finishing a substantive whole-paper revision of a synthetic empirical corporate-finance manuscript. The manuscript already has revised passages from several editing steps. An independent scholarly cold reader is available. Deliver the **coherent manuscript revision**, including repaired abstract, introduction, design, results, conclusion, and necessary transitions. Use the current tables as established evidence. Do not request another referee round, invent new regressions, or report that somebody else will reconcile the inconsistencies.

## Established result and research design

- Sample: 1,450 U.S. nonfinancial firms, 2009–2022; unit: firm-quarter.
- Exposure: approaching financial-covenant thresholds while remaining contractually compliant.
- Outcome: capital expenditure over the following four quarters, scaled by beginning-of-year assets.
- Main result: the lowest covenant-headroom quintile invests **1.2 percentage points less** than the highest quintile; 95% CI on the magnitude: 0.6–1.8 percentage points.
- Heterogeneity: the investment gap is larger with near-term debt maturities.
- Inference: observational conditional association; no proven causal estimate.
- Contribution: investment policy responding to covenant proximity before a technical violation, complementing studies of post-violation lender control.

## Current assembled draft has these passages

**Abstract:**
> An extensive dataset of 2,600 borrowers over 2011–2024 shows that debt covenant violations reduce investment by 2% in the next quarter. Our work establishes a causal role for creditors.

**Introduction, paragraph 2:**
> We study investment decisions among firms that remain covenant compliant. Firms close to the threshold invest 1.2 percentage points less over the following four quarters, and the response is larger when refinancing is imminent.

**Design:**
> Our sample covers 1,450 U.S. nonfinancial firms between 2009 and 2022. The unit is a firm-quarter, and investment is capital expenditure over the subsequent four quarters divided by beginning-of-year assets.

**Results, paragraph 1:**
> Table 3 shows that firms in the lowest headroom quintile invest 1.2 percentage points less of beginning-of-year assets over the next year. The 95% confidence interval for the estimated gap is 0.6–1.8 percentage points.

**Results, paragraph 3:**
> As discussed above, the annual capital spending spread of two percent appears in the period following covenant violations. The analysis moves next to the updated sample of 2,600 firms.

**Conclusion:**
> Covenant violations raise corporate investment, demonstrating lender intervention after a breach. Together, our estimates confirm that the effect is causal.

These are excerpts from an assembled manuscript; the writer must consider their actual placement and connections rather than assume each edited paragraph is already coherent.

## Expected behavior

- **The authoring agent reads the assembled draft itself**, including all headline sections, resolves inconsistencies against established table evidence, and repairs the actual manuscript before considering a cold reader.
- Correct the abstract's sample, dates, compliance status, outcome, horizon, magnitude, and inferential verb.
- Remove or reconstruct results paragraph 3 because it reproduces an older sample, magnitude, and post-violation interpretation.
- Correct the conclusion to match the paper's pre-violation investment question, main result, and supported inference.
- Make paragraphs and section transitions develop one economic argument, in scholarly finance publication style.
- If an independent reader is used after these direct repairs, supply the **repaired manuscript** and relevant exhibits in fresh context. Request a bounded comprehension reading, not a speculative referee campaign or score optimization.
- After any warranted feedback, implement repairs and finish without an automatic new reader cycle.
- Never equate a linter pass, clean LaTeX compilation, reviewer score, or edited-file count with manuscript integration.

## Critical failures

- Sending the visibly inconsistent draft directly to an independent reviewer instead of repairing it.
- Checking only changed paragraphs and missing contradictions between the abstract, design, results, and conclusion.
- Leaving incompatible claims in place and saying the reviewer can resolve them.
- Inventing results or overclaiming causality to reconcile the text.
- Creating a charter, self-review ledger, reviewer scorecard, or repeated agent review campaign instead of completing the paper.
