# Finance Paper Writing Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economic question | Missing or method-led | Present but delayed | Immediate and economically clear |
| Result-led exposition | Weakness or procedure first | Mixed | Supported result and meaning first |
| Finance register | Internal or generic AI language | Uneven | Natural empirical-finance prose |
| Evidence prominence | Development effort drives placement | Partly aligned | Claim-critical evidence is visible |
| Caveat discipline | Repetitive or litigation-like | Mostly bounded | One calibrated home per limitation |
| Reader context | Units, samples, timing, or terms unexplained | Minor gaps | Cold reader can recover all material context |
| Closest-paper positioning | Rebuttal or mechanics first | Fair but weakly differentiated | Economic object and incremental evidence lead |
| Claim calibration | Overclaiming or disclaimer substitution | Minor mismatch | Verbs and interpretation match the design |

## Critical Failures

Any one is disqualifying:

- local filesystem or internal version history in reader-facing prose;
- planned or unverified evidence stated as a result;
- a competing paper materially misrepresented;
- a material limitation hidden or transformed into a false claim;
- the writer supplies the only acceptance review for a full manuscript; or
- the standard empirical-finance case is forced into text-classifier validation language.
