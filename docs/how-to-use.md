# How to Use

## Whole paper

> Use finance-paper-writing to revise this empirical finance paper. Make the economic argument and sharpest evidence clear, preserve claim calibration, and keep the abstract, introduction, results, and conclusion coherent.

The agent should read the current manuscript and relevant established evidence, construct and implement the requested economic argument and prose (including introduction and contribution), check affected quantitative claims against cited exhibits and literature statements against sources, compile edited LaTeX, and report the actual change. Argument and evidence order deserve attention before sentence polishing. A new charter, run directory, scorecard, or reviewer campaign is not required.

## Section or passage

> Revise the introduction so a finance reader understands the tension, question, main finding, and contribution after the first two pages.

> Rewrite this abstract as connected prose. Keep the sharpest quantitative evidence and explain its economic meaning.

> Rewrite this paragraph in empirical-finance register while preserving the supported claim.

Read the affected section and transitions after editing. Scale checks to the substantive changes; do not promote a paragraph revision into a full research restart.

## Review when requested

> Read this paper and identify the few structural problems that prevent its argument from being understood. Do not edit or start new empirical work.

A bounded reader assessment is useful when requested or when it answers a concrete unresolved structural question. Distinguish demonstrated defects, verification questions, and optional extensions. The latter two do not automatically become manuscript caveats.

## Text-based measures

Use the optional text-measure reference when presenting a text construct. Describe the established measure and its actual validity evidence. A writing task does not authorize another source-reading, classifier, or coding cycle.

## Authorial voice and economic contribution

Where effective author-written passages already exist, use `references/authorial-voice.md` to preserve the author's stance, argument progression, evidence treatment, and natural finance register. Draft the requested section yourself using the present study's evidence. No stand-alone voice profile, source-collection campaign, or approval cycle is needed. A reusable voice guide is optional when the author explicitly requests one.

For a substantive introduction or results revision, connect the economically important unresolved question, plausible mechanisms, decisive quantitative comparison, and incremental insight rather than merely polishing prose or listing estimates. Reconcile the affected magnitudes, units, uncertainty, and exhibit references directly with established results.

## GPT/Codex anti-defensive guidance

For every finance-manuscript prose revision by Codex or any GPT-family model, read and apply `references/anti-defensive-prose.md` even when editing a single sentence. When underlying model identity is unknown, apply it. Confirmed non-GPT/non-Codex agents may use it selectively. Apply the standard during writing and check the affected passage; no separate audit report is needed.

## Optional formal full-paper records

The executable initializer `skill/finance-paper-writing/scripts/init_run.py` creates the structured files, pass log, and manuscript baseline for an explicitly requested formal full-paper pass. Its default mode is `full-manuscript`; other modes are retained for legacy use. The validator checks a selected package's record schema. Ordinary prose revision proceeds directly from the manuscript and its established evidence. The formal workflow remains optional even for a whole-paper revision and cannot override a project's prohibition on gates, audits, or repeated reviews.

## Report back

Explain the substantive argument, interpretation, and passages improved in self-contained prose. Link the revised manuscript or compiled PDF when relevant. Mention a remaining consequential research decision directly. Do not substitute worklogs or process status for the paper.
