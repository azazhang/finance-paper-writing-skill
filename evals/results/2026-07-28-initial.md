# Initial Skill Evaluation

## Scope

Fresh-agent dry runs covered:

- Case 2: defense-first closest-paper comparison; and
- Case 4: standard corporate-finance introduction and design.

Deterministic tests separately covered development leakage, disclaimer repetition, TeX dependency traversal, stale reviews, reviewer independence, pass separation, linter dispositions, and cold-reader score thresholds.

## First Dry-Run Findings

The first Case 4 response selected `paragraph-edit` even though the prompt named two manuscript sections. It also stated the observational boundary twice. The skill was revised to:

- reserve paragraph mode for one isolated passage with no section-level judgment;
- route named sections to `section-revision`; and
- require one primary home for the causal boundary.

The second Case 4 response selected `section-revision`, used no text-measure vocabulary, and stated the observational boundary once.

The first Case 2 response removed implementation details from the opening but replaced them with another negative construction: “the contribution does not rest on.” The skill was revised to:

- require a positive opening grounded in the neighboring economic object;
- prohibit equivalent negative-contribution framing; and
- withhold final comparison prose when comparator facts are missing.

The final Case 2 response supplied a missing-evidence checklist and positive outline without inventing facts or drafting another defensive opening.

## Result

The evaluations identified real instruction failures rather than merely confirming the initial design. Both failures were converted into skill rules, linter coverage where appropriate, and regression expectations.

Further evaluation across complete manuscripts and finance subfields remains necessary before claiming broad external validity.
