# Finance Paper Writing

**A paper succeeds by making a clear, falsifiable economic argument and putting its sharpest quantitative evidence front and center.** Write in the **scholarly publication register of financial economics**, as expected in rigorous journal articles and finance seminars. Explain what the evidence teaches finance, with claims calibrated to the design. The discipline's norms for economic reasoning and academic exposition govern before individual author preferences. A polished catalogue of analyses or qualifications is still a weak paper.

Apply this guidance throughout the assigned revision. Current user instructions, the agreed research plan, and project restrictions control. This skill supplies editorial judgment; it does not authorize new analysis, repeated reviews, or an alternative agenda. **The agent is responsible for developing and writing the economic argument, including the introduction, contribution, and interpretation when assigned.** Use the author's established question, evidence, and direction to exercise substantive editorial judgment and produce the draft itself. Identify a genuinely consequential unresolved scientific choice when necessary; do not defer writing to the author or substitute an outline of possible arguments for the requested prose.

**Mandatory anti-defensive guidance for GPT/Codex:** When the agent is Codex or runs on a GPT-family model, read [anti-defensive-prose.md](references/anti-defensive-prose.md) and apply its standards throughout **every** finance-manuscript prose edit, even a one-sentence correction or a revision with no explicitly defensive language. If the model family is uncertain, apply the guidance. Agents that know they are neither GPT-family nor Codex may use this reference selectively. Apply the standards through the writing itself; do not launch a separate audit, review cycle, or compliance artifact to demonstrate adherence.

## Start with the paper, then revise it

Announce use of this skill. Recover the current manuscript, the author's request, and the established evidence needed for that request. Keep the following in working context: the economic question, tension or mechanism, main finding and magnitude, design, closest contribution, and the passage being improved. Use the existing research state rather than creating a new charter or inventory every time work resumes.

Scale effort to the revision. Edit an isolated passage directly. A section revision needs its argument, relevant evidence, and surrounding transitions. A whole-paper revision needs coherent abstract, introduction, exhibits, results, and conclusion. **After substantial whole-paper writing, the writing agent must read the actual assembled manuscript in full and repair inconsistencies, patchwork transitions, and contradictions before considering a separate reader.** This is part of writing the paper, with no required run directory, ledger, scorecard, reviewer campaign, or acceptance certificate.

Read [finance-prose-rubric.md](references/finance-prose-rubric.md) and [voice-calibration.md](references/voice-calibration.md) as **core standards for substantive finance-manuscript writing**. The latter calibrates to verified JF/JFE/RFS publication conventions across research designs; personal authorial voice is secondary. Their distilled standards suffice in ordinary work; do not start an exemplar-reading campaign. For GPT/Codex, the anti-defensive reference above is also always active on **every** prose edit. Read other references selectively when needed. The [model passages](examples/before-after.md) illustrate original, synthetic finance prose. [multi-pass-adherence.md](references/multi-pass-adherence.md) explains proportionate revision and the optional formal full-paper workflow.

## Make an economic argument

The question is about an economically meaningful behavior, friction, allocation, or price. A regression specification, dataset, disclosure category, or institutional fact is not itself the question. The paper must advance an answer: decide what the reader should believe, why the economic mechanism yields that claim, which quantitative comparison most strongly supports it, and how it changes understanding relative to the closest literature. Use those judgments to write the argument directly; they do not require a new template or planning artifact. A claim-free summary or list of results is an unsuccessful manuscript deliverable, even when its individual statements are accurate.

Build the argument around a concrete economic puzzle, competing force, unresolved prediction, or consequential gap in knowledge. Explain what is at stake for financing, investment, risk sharing, asset prices, or allocation and why the existing evidence leaves the answer interesting. Identify what the paper's strongest empirical comparison actually resolves, which alternatives remain viable, and what a finance reader learns as a result. Where theory supplies competing predictions, use them to explain why the comparison discriminates; do not manufacture disagreement solely to create a narrative.

A new measure, dataset, model, or significant coefficient earns scholarly relevance through the economic question and inference it enables. State the resulting conceptual contribution directly, distinguishing it from the closest actual literature. Write the economic answer in the manuscript itself, including the introduction and contribution when assigned. Calibrate it to the evidence without making the reader infer the answer from a sequence of tests. These judgments are part of writing, not a preliminary charter, approval gate, or new empirical-work authorization.

“Falsifiable” means that an economic claim can be confronted with evidence. It does not demand an axiom, theorem, deductive certainty, or a causal estimator for every contribution. An informative empirical characterization, predictive relation, mechanism comparison, or causal result can support an argument at its appropriate scope. Uncertainty calibrates that argument; it must not erase the claim or become the paper's central message. Several complementary comparisons may support one integrated interpretation.

Explain the competing forces and what observation would distinguish the paper's interpretation from a plausible alternative. State a directional prediction when the theory supplies one; do not invent formal hypotheses to decorate exploratory research. Organize completed assets by their role in that argument, rather than when they were produced or how much effort they consumed.

The first two pages should make clear what the paper asks, why a finance reader should care, what the paper does, the sharpest evidence, and how that evidence changes understanding. If the argument is obscure, rebuild its order before polishing individual sentences. Do not shrink the contribution around a superficial literature comparison. Use finance-literature-review when the revision requires new literature work; represent neighboring papers' actual economic objects and evidence fairly.

## Write an abstract a reader can understand

Write connected prose with a causal logic the reader can follow:

- the economic tension and question;
- the setting, measure, or identifying variation that makes the question answerable;
- the principal finding with one or a few interpretable magnitudes;
- the economic implication.

This is a useful sequence, not a sentence-count template. Keep secondary sample counts, model labels, specification catalogues, and technical qualifications in the paper. A list of percentages from disconnected subsamples leaves the reader to supply the argument. Choose quantitative evidence for its centrality and interpretability; including every estimate weakens the abstract.

Read the finished abstract as ordinary prose. Can a finance reader explain what the paper does and what it teaches after one reading? If not, rewrite the abstract rather than appending another result. Preserve an effective coauthor draft when it states the argument better; improve factual precision without dismantling its logic or authorial voice.

## Give each exhibit an economic job

Select main-text evidence that establishes, distinguishes, or materially qualifies the paper's argument. The Appendix is for relevant supporting evidence and detail, not an archive of all attempted tests. Preserve baseline and material exploratory specifications in the research record, including uncertainty and selection decisions.

An inconclusive exploratory coefficient does not earn a manuscript table merely because it was estimated. A negative or null effect is a finding when credible, sufficiently precise evidence supports the relevant no-effect prediction or otherwise materially informs the economic question. Retain evidence that changes the interpretation of a reported claim, and change the claim when warranted. Do not select exhibits solely by statistical significance, hide contrary evidence, or mechanically discard informative comparisons because a simultaneous interval spans zero.

Make the decisive exhibit visible and explain its economic magnitude, unit, timing, comparison, and uncertainty. Distinguish descriptive, associational, predictive, mechanism-consistent, and causal evidence through the design and chosen verbs. [evidence-and-claims.md](references/evidence-and-claims.md) gives placement and claim guidance.

## Write as an author

Default paragraph logic is **economic point → evidence → interpretation**, followed by a bounded qualification when needed. Vary this order when the passage reads more clearly another way. Results prose interprets evidence; it does not narrate each table column or the research process. For a contribution paragraph, connect **unresolved economic question → what the evidence establishes → what changes in understanding** without formulaic announcements of novelty.

Lead with the supported claim. Use the strongest verb earned by the design, such as documents, is associated with, predicts, or reduces. Give each material identification or measurement qualification one primary home where it changes inference. Elsewhere, calibrated verbs carry the scope. Preserve substantive uncertainty and contrary evidence while removing repeated self-protection.

Do not negotiate with an imagined referee in the abstract, contribution, result openings, or conclusion. Optional extensions and speculative objections do not become limitations by default. A verified problem calls for repairing the claim, design explanation, or evidence; a protective sentence is rarely the repair. See [anti-defensive-prose.md](references/anti-defensive-prose.md).

Explain a neighboring paper's contribution before distinguishing the present paper. Different data can reveal a mechanism, challenge a conventional interpretation, or resolve an economic question; novelty need not be confined to a new method. See [closest-paper-positioning.md](references/closest-paper-positioning.md).

## Maintain finance register on every revision

Use firms, managers, investors, lenders, portfolios, prices, cash flows, and financing choices as sentence subjects. Define financial objects, denominators, timing, and units before interpreting them. Use ordinary finance headings that identify the economic content, such as Data and Measurement, Investment, or Financing Adjustment.

Translate internal development language into descriptions of the actual research. Headings such as “Financial definitions” or “Economic coverage,” when merely labels for internal organization, obscure what the reader will learn. Do not apply a word blacklist: source, historical, deployment, and similar words can be appropriate in context. Judge syntax and economic meaning rather than replacing tokens mechanically.

Maintain the same standard after adding a table, incorporating a review, or making a later correction. An earlier prose pass does not protect later revisions from drift. Reread the affected paragraph or section as connected prose after editing; a successful word substitution says little about its argument. Apply the disciplinary [voice-calibration](references/voice-calibration.md) standard in every substantive revision. Where effective author-approved prose exists, preserve its natural terminology, rhythm, and argument within the scholarly finance register. Do not substitute generic AI exposition for this disciplinary style.

## Revise efficiently and stop

For a broad revision, separate intellectual jobs when useful: argument and evidence order first, interpretation and scholarly finance register next, then numbers, layout, and the **writer's integrated reading of the assembled paper**. “One pass, one job” is a way to focus attention, not a requirement to produce eight logs, repeated independent reviews, or another process record.

**Writer's integrated reading is required for a substantial whole-paper draft or rewrite.** After assembling the revision, read the **whole current manuscript in its actual reading order**, preferably the rendered PDF, with main exhibits and notes where referenced. Track the central argument from abstract and introduction through evidence and conclusion. Repair conflicting claims, samples, constructs, units, estimates, horizons, mechanism descriptions, and stitched-together paragraphs or transitions **in the paper itself**. Then reread the repaired portions and their connections. A skimming checklist, an account of previous editing, or an independent reader's feedback does not fulfill this responsibility. For a local edit, read only the affected section or passage and its necessary surrounding context. See [independent-reader.md](references/independent-reader.md).

Use existing results to check changed quantitative claims against the cited tables or figures: sample, outcome, coefficient or magnitude, scale, horizon, uncertainty, and exhibit reference. Check changed literature attributions against the actual source. For a broad revision, keep the headline claim coherent across title, abstract, introduction, main exhibits, and conclusion; for a small edit, check only affected claims and transitions. Compile edited LaTeX with the available compiler and inspect affected pages when layout matters. Ordinary computational traceability is sufficient unless a formal audit is explicitly assigned.

**Only after the writer's own integrated repair**, use one bounded [independent scholarly cold reading](references/independent-reader.md) when requested or materially useful. For a substantial whole-paper reconstruction, it is a normal final editorial aid if a genuinely separate reader is permitted and available. The reader reconstructs the economic question, evidence, answer, and contribution from the manuscript alone, then identifies a few demonstrated communication defects; it does not act as an automatic scientific referee or score the paper toward publication acceptance. The writer resolves substantiated feedback and checks affected prose. Do not launch serial reviewer rounds, pursue score thresholds, or let unavailable independent reading block the manuscript. Finish when the requested substantive revision is implemented and checked; do not prolong it with taste-driven rewrites or process artifacts.

## Report the substantive change

Give a concise, self-contained explanation of the argument, evidence interpretation, and manuscript passages improved, with a link to the revised manuscript or compiled PDF when relevant. State a material unresolved research decision directly. Do not replace the report with Markdown worklogs, gate tables, linter results, file counts, or claims that the skill was followed. The revised paper is the deliverable.
