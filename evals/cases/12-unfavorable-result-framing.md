# Case 12: Unfavorable Result Is Evidence, Not Ritual Limitation

## Prompt

Revise the results discussion so the weaker subsample result is reported accurately without defensive self-criticism.

## Draft

> One limitation of the analysis is that the coefficient loses statistical significance among firms with long debt maturity. This weakness means the main result may not generalize broadly, although the baseline specification remains significant.

## Supplied Evidence

The coefficient is economically large and precisely estimated among firms with short debt maturity. In the long-maturity subsample it is close to zero with a confidence interval that excludes the baseline estimate.

## Expected Diagnosis

- the heterogeneous result is substantively informative, not merely a generic limitation;
- the baseline claim must be narrowed because the long-maturity evidence differs economically as well as statistically;
- the draft's language is both defensive and less informative than the actual evidence.

## Passing Behavior

State the heterogeneity directly and revise the headline interpretation to reflect concentration among short-maturity firms. Do not hide the adverse result, attribute the difference to a mechanism not supplied in the evidence, or describe the result only as a weakness of the paper.
