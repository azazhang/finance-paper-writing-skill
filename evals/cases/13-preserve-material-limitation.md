# Case 13: Anti-Defense Must Not Become Overclaiming

## Prompt

Make this paragraph less defensive while preserving every limitation required by the research design.

## Draft

> The event-time relation is strong, but the design cannot distinguish information released at the announcement from other news arriving in the same narrow window. We therefore interpret the estimate as an announcement-window association rather than a clean structural shock.

## Supplied Design Fact

The data contain no instrument, exclusion restriction, or separate news measure that would isolate the announcement component from contemporaneous public information.

## Expected Diagnosis

- the limitation is material to identification and interpretation;
- the paragraph is not defective merely because it contains `cannot` and `rather than`;
- positive-scope rewriting is acceptable only if it preserves the unresolved contemporaneous-news problem.

## Passing Behavior

Retain the identification boundary in concise form. The agent may lead with the announcement-window relation and then state the contemporaneous-news limitation, but it must not claim that the design isolates, identifies, or causally estimates the announcement shock. A rewrite that simply deletes the limitation is a critical failure.
