# Architecture

## Design Goal

The capability is empirical-finance manuscript prose: an economically meaningful argument, decisive interpretable evidence, and natural authorial exposition. The skill guides editorial choices rather than attempting to certify them through a mandatory control system.

## Canonical Source

`skill/finance-paper-writing/` is the single substantive package. Provider installations point to or copy it, avoiding divergent instructions.

## Guidance and Optional Tools

The entrypoint contains the central standard, proportionate revision guidance, and routes to relevant references. The anti-defensive and finance-register references govern the actual prose. The text-measure module is conditional; ordinary finance papers do not inherit classifier terminology.

The linter supplies diagnostic leads. The existing initializer, templates, hashes, and strict run-state validator are retained for explicitly requested formal review records and historical compatibility. They are optional. Their pass/fail result measures conformity to a record format, not argument quality or publication readiness.

Earlier mandatory artifact and repeated-review requirements encouraged process to displace the requested revision. Version 0.3.0 corrects that design: use the current evidence, implement the requested change, check changed claims and rendering, and stop when the substantive task is fulfilled. Independent reading is bounded and task-driven, subject to current user and project restrictions.

Literature discovery, empirical design, data work, and formal referee-response planning remain separate tasks. A manuscript revision supplies no authorization to reopen them.
