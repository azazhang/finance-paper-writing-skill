# Agentic Manuscript Reading and Optional Independent Reader

The writing agent is responsible for validating its own scholarly manuscript against [the main skill](../SKILL.md) and [finance publication voice](voice-calibration.md). Validation is an **actual reading and substantive repair**, with no mechanical script, certificate, form, score, or compulsory review round.

## Writer's integrated reading

After a substantial full-paper draft or rewrite, read the **assembled manuscript in order**, preferably the rendered PDF, from abstract through conclusion and with the principal exhibits where they are cited. Inspect the paper the finance reader will see; a summary of edited files or a skim of the abstract cannot substitute.

Read for the manuscript's economic spine and for inconsistencies across sections: the question, research object, sample, units, variable definitions, timing, results, mechanism, and contribution must agree. Detect contradictory estimates, old claims, duplicated paragraphs, abrupt transitions, unexplained referents, disconnected coefficient catalogues, defensive prose, and generic AI language. Verify important statements against existing evidence and citations. **Repair the actual manuscript** and reread the affected transitions; when restructuring is broad, reread the assembled result.

For section work, examine the complete section and consequential neighboring passages. For a small edit, inspect the affected paragraph and immediate context. Match effort to the task. No written self-review report is required.

## One bounded fresh reading when useful

Only after the writer's own repairs, a separate reader may assess a substantial whole-paper reconstruction when available, permitted, and likely to add value, or when the author requests it. Give the reader the finished manuscript and relevant exhibits with **minimal development history**. Ask the reader to reconstruct from the text:

- the consequential economic question and its motivation;
- the design or comparison and its inferential scope;
- the central quantitative or theoretical finding and its interpretation;
- the paper's incremental contribution to the closest literature.

Then ask for the **few demonstrated problems** that materially impair a scholarly finance reader's understanding. Each criticism should identify the relevant passage, what it communicates, and why revision is warranted. A missing problem need not be invented. Scores, journal-acceptance guesses, speculative robustness demands, and an obligatory minimum number of criticisms are unhelpful.

The writer evaluates feedback independently: repair verified writing defects; investigate concrete factual questions; treat extra analyses and alternative paper directions as optional new work. Reread the revised text and **stop**. A second independent read is warranted only by an explicit request or a substantial reconstruction that invalidates the first reading.

An explicitly requested scientific referee assessment of identification, measurement, and contribution is a different task. It must not be silently attached to an ordinary writing revision.
