# Authorial Voice from Exemplary Writing

## Purpose and activation

Use this guidance when the assigned task involves the author's voice, introduction, contribution, interpretation, or substantial manuscript rewriting, particularly when strong author-written passages are already available. This is a **selective reference**, not a separate stage of every revision. For a small factual or grammatical correction, preserve the surrounding voice and make the correction directly.

The agent is responsible for drafting compelling finance prose, including intellectually demanding introduction and contribution passages. Prior author writing provides an empirical guide to style; it does not relieve the agent of constructing the economic argument from the present study's established evidence.

## Choose authentic exemplars

Prefer, in order: a passage the author explicitly approves; effective passages in the current manuscript that the author wants preserved; prior author-written finance manuscripts available in the project; and coauthor-approved passages when the current assignment treats them as stylistic models. Prioritize examples serving a comparable rhetorical job, such as an introduction opening, a contribution paragraph, or an empirical-results interpretation.

Use the text already in context or project assets. Request extra exemplars only when voice fidelity is central to the assigned task and no suitable existing writing is available. Never attribute synthetic skill examples, generic model prose, or an unrelated scholar's wording to the author. Do not infer an author's characteristic style from a single accidental phrasing.

## Infer a concise, task-relevant voice specification

Observe these properties silently while reading enough text to serve the revision:

- **Economic stance and claim strength:** how the author motivates a financial friction, introduces an unresolved question, and states an answer supported by evidence.
- **Argument progression:** how an opening moves from economic stakes to question, empirical comparison, findings, and contribution.
- **Evidence presentation:** how the author places figures, units, uncertainty, examples, mechanism comparisons, and citations in connected prose.
- **Sentence and paragraph rhythm:** typical sentence length and variation, transitions, paragraph density, use of first person, and where technical detail appears.
- **Finance vocabulary:** preferred descriptions of agents, contracts, markets, financial objects, and inferential status.

Use the few patterns that materially improve the passage. Keep them in working context rather than generating a voice dossier, database, scorecard, or required profile. A reusable written style note may be created only when the author explicitly asks for one.

## Draft in the author's voice

Write the assigned substantive passage yourself. Preserve strong existing prose unless revision is needed for the economic argument, factual accuracy, or coherence. When restructuring, retain the author's distinctive reasoning and cadence while advancing the sharpest supported claim. Translate methods into economic objects and interpret the decisive comparison in terms relevant to financial economics.

Style follows substance. Respect the actual study's sample, estimand, magnitudes, limitations, and closest literature even when an exemplar uses stronger rhetoric or a different design. An authorial phrase from a different paper carries no evidentiary authority into the present paper.

For GPT-family models and Codex, the mandatory [anti-defensive guidance](anti-defensive-prose.md) applies alongside authorial voice. Preserve meaningful conceptual contrasts and real inferential boundaries, while avoiding mechanical antitheses, hedging stacks, and generic assertions of importance.

## Compare and improve the finished passage

Read the edited passage as connected prose, alongside a relevant exemplar only if one was already available. Assess whether it communicates the same kind of economic reasoning, preserves the author's natural register, and teaches the reader something material about the study. Revise the passage itself when needed. Do not launch a separate reviewer, score the resemblance, or require author approval of each sentence.

If the author requests a reusable voice guide, produce a compact set of observed tendencies with short **author-provided** examples and distinguish direct observations from editorial preferences. Keep that optional guide subordinate to the manuscript's economic argument and evidentiary discipline.

*Methodological inspiration: Manish Jha, “AI for Finance Research,” especially the discussion of deriving writing guidance from exemplary author passages: https://mjha91.github.io/pdf/AIFinanceResearch.pdf . This module adapts the idea to a manuscript-first finance-writing workflow.*
