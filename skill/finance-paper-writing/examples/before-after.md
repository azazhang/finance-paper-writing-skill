# Generalized Before-and-After Examples

These examples are synthetic and intentionally generic.

## Development Record to Research Description

Before:

> The project combines two locally archived loan files. After the v3-clean observations are produced, the pipeline merges them onto the Compustat backbone.

After:

> I combine DealScan loan facilities with Compustat accounting data and link borrowers by historical identifiers and facility dates.

Why: the revision describes sources, observations, and linkage rather than the researcher’s computer and model history.

## Weakness-First to Result-First

Before:

> The annual design cannot establish that the disclosure causes subsequent volatility.

After:

> Firms discussing the focal condition subsequently experience higher idiosyncratic volatility, conditional on firm characteristics and pre-disclosure risk.

Why: the calibrated verb and timing state what the evidence supports. The design section can explain the noncausal boundary once.

## Comparator Air Defense to Economic Positioning

Before:

> Perfect nesting should not be expected because the measures use different sources, filters, and denominators, so symmetric agreement is not the appropriate test.

After:

> Prior work measures firms’ exposure to a specific operating constraint. This paper studies a broader disturbance to the same organizational input and tests whether it contains information beyond the narrower condition.

Why: the neighboring economic object and incremental question precede measurement mechanics.

## Generic Template to Venue-Relevant Content

Before:

> This section reports research ethics, AI responsibility, author contributions, funding, and conflicts of interest.

After:

> Omit the section when the venue does not require it and none of the items changes interpretation of the study. Preserve any required records outside the manuscript.

Why: administrative completeness is venue-specific, not a universal manuscript contribution.

## Standard Empirical Finance Paper

Before:

> The data pipeline deploys three cleaning stages before constructing the treatment.

After:

> I exclude financial firms, require nonmissing accounting controls, and define treatment using covenant thresholds measured at the fiscal-quarter end.

Why: the revision remains useful outside text-based measurement papers.

## Asset-Pricing Result

Before:

> It should be noted that the factor is not intended to prove a behavioral mechanism, and the portfolio evidence cannot eliminate every alternative explanation.

After:

> The long–short portfolio earns a positive average return that remains after controlling for standard equity factors. The evidence establishes incremental return predictability; it does not by itself distinguish behavioral from risk-based explanations.

Why: the result and inferential boundary appear once and in that order.

## Banking Sample Context

Before:

> The bank sample is redeclared after several pipeline screens, and the robustness table uses the final backbone.

After:

> The sample includes U.S. commercial banks with nonmissing Call Report capital, liquidity, and loan-growth variables. Each specification states the additional restriction responsible for changes in sample size.

Why: a reader needs the population, variables, and restriction—not the order in which code produced them.
