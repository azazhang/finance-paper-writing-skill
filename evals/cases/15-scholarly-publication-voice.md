# Case 15: Scholarly Finance Publication Voice Over Generic AI Prose

## Prompt

Rewrite the introduction opening below as the first two paragraphs of an empirical corporate-finance journal article. Use only the established synthetic evidence. Produce the actual paper prose, not a style analysis or review notes. Apply the core scholarly finance voice calibration; do not mimic a named academic or use generic popular-academic rhetoric.

## Draft

> In today's dynamic financial landscape, debt covenants play an increasingly important and multifaceted role in shaping corporate strategic decision-making. This study offers a novel lens into the complex interplay between firm behavior, financial flexibility, and covenant mechanisms. By leveraging a comprehensive empirical dataset and employing a robust regression framework, we aim to shed light on these critical relationships. The results underscore the profound importance of financial contracting for companies and stakeholders.

## Established synthetic facts

- Question: whether firms reduce investment as financial covenant headroom shrinks, before formal violations.
- Economic mechanism under study: anticipation of restricted refinancing capacity may affect investment even while debt covenants are satisfied.
- Sample: nonfinancial U.S. firms; firm-quarter observations with measured financial covenant headroom and subsequent capital spending.
- Main evidence: lowest versus highest headroom quintile is associated with 1.2 percentage points less investment relative to beginning-of-year assets over the next four quarters, conditional on firm and calendar-quarter effects and observed financial controls.
- Mechanism-consistent heterogeneity: the investment difference is larger for firms with substantial debt maturing within one year.
- Inferential boundary: observational association; the reported specification does not establish an exogenous change in covenant headroom.
- Closest literature: prior studies of formal covenant violations and lender intervention. Do not invent specific papers, numerical estimates, or citations.

## Successful response

- Opens with the concrete economic question and its relation to borrower behavior and financing conditions.
- Explains why an effect before formal violation is an economically consequential margin.
- States the comparison, timing, magnitude, and coherent interpretation.
- Explains the contribution relative to the stated neighboring research without inventing citation details.
- Uses natural financial-economics publication prose throughout; no sweeping claims about a dynamic landscape, novel lenses, leveraging data, or stakeholders.
- Preserves observational inference and the genuine uncertainty about causation without ritual disclaimer cascades.
- The agent writes the revised paragraphs itself and does not propose a voice-profile exercise, mock referee review, or new estimation work.

## Critical failures

- The output is a polished general essay with few financial-economic objects, measures, comparisons, or interpreted results.
- It presents the novelty of the dataset or methods as the primary economic contribution.
- It asserts that covenant proximity causally reduces investment based on the given evidence.
- It replaces the requested paragraphs with an editorial checklist or generic review comments.
